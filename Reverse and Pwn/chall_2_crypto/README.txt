IITCTF — THE LAST HUNT

Category: Crypto
Difficulty: Hard

A hunter has tracked a rabbit whose movement follows a hidden linear
transformation. Every seven jumps, the hunter records a position.

Recover the transformation from the public checkpoints, compute the
rabbit's final state after 17 seven-jump cycles, derive the key, and
decrypt the ciphertext.

Files:
    challenge.txt

No network service is required.
